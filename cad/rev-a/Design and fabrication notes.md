## Packaging and fit

- **Width.** Mount faces are 200 mm apart. Each tire starts 7 mm outboard and ends at 140 mm from center. Each guard starts 3 mm beyond the nominal tread and is 2 mm thick. W = 200 + 2(7 + 33 + 3 + 2) = **290 mm**. Check actual tire runout, shoulder bulge, loaded flex and guard deflection. The 3 mm face rail gives 4 mm to the inboard tire before screw heads.
- **250 mm scenario.** With opposing motors, W = 2B + g + 90 mm, where B is motor reach from the mounting face including terminals and routing, and g is central loom clearance. At B = 76.5 mm, the stack is already 243 mm before wiring or loom space. At B = 86 mm and g = 20 mm it is 282 mm. This design provides 28 mm between ends at that reach. The 250 mm cap would allow B ≤ 70 mm with the same 20 mm gap. A narrower class would require a different stack, e.g. shorter hardware, staggered motors, reduced tire/guard width, or a combination.
- **Length.** 62 mm pitch gives 5 mm nominal inter-tire gap and a 181 mm tire train. A 200 mm cage gives only 19 mm total ahead of and behind the tires. This layout gives 49.5 mm behind and 61.5 mm ahead. Each pack lays across its own rear bay. Confirm real connector exits and removal path.
- **Height.** Nominal underfloor clearance 5 mm, can bottom 10.1 mm, keeper top 48.5 mm, electronics deck underside 50 mm, driver top 98 mm and lid underside 103 mm. The 1.5 mm keeper-to-deck and 5 mm driver-to-lid clearances are static geometry values. Include protruding heads, wires and flex in the fit check.
- **Hub.** Team motor STEP has a shaft tip 21.5 mm beyond the face including a 6 mm boss. With the rim at 7 mm, theoretical engagement is 14.5 mm. The Ø6 round motor proxy overlaps the undersize D-bore. Verify the actual flat, usable engagement and repeatable seating. Prove the retention with pull and reversing torque tests.
- **Motor fixing.** The 3 mm rail locates the gearbox boss in a Ø12.6 mm opening. Transfer-drill the actual gearbox mounting holes from a real motor and use the specified screw depth. Jig the rails while joining each flange strip and check all six axle heights before tightening the floor bolts. The printed cradle and cushioned keeper restrain can motion. Their detailed fastener geometry needs the physical fit check. Motor torque reaction travels through the steel face fixings.

## Scoop and manufacturing

The 282 mm wide scoop has a 12 mm level toe, followed by projected runs of 19, 16 and 11 mm. Their pitches are 14°, 34° and 58° with bend rotations of 14°, 20° and 24°. The inside radius is provisionally 2 mm. The toe underside is nominally 1.5 mm above level ground and the scoop top reaches 36.13 mm. The three ribs carry loads to bolted feet. Fit the scoop to the ribs in a jig before choosing the final joint detail. The upper closure is separate. Check whether the local brake can reach the short toe and all three bends without a tooling clash. Derive the blank only after a coupon establishes bend deduction for the actual stock and tools. [Press brake flange and tooling limits](https://sendcutsend.com/guidelines/bend-deformation/)

At 90 mm ahead of the front axle, 1.5 mm is consumed by about 0.95° nose-down pitch or by 1.5 mm of uniform tire compression. Start by measuring clearance with the loaded bot and test over the worst arena seam. If needed, adjust the three feet to give 2–3 mm loaded clearance. Deburr and round the toe and corners to protect the ring. Side armor starts 7 mm above nominal ground and rises to 52 mm, with tire tops at 57 mm. Check sideways tire deflection against the guard. Detail the rear tabs, guard-angle holes and removable panel joints with the actual fasteners.

For a first-pass strip check, a 20 mm wide strip of 1.5 mm steel, idealized as a 20 mm cantilever, has Z = 7.5 mm³ and I = 5.625 mm⁴. A 50 N concentrated normal edge load gives about 133 MPa and 0.12 mm elastic deflection with E = 200 GPa. At 100 N the idealized stress is about 267 MPa. Actual contact width, bends, ribs and joining change the result. Test center and corner loads. Compare the three-bend scoop with a straight wedge at the same loaded lip height and mass. The current formed solid has editable radii and section geometry. Production flats and bend settings are pending. Use PETG with washers and layer direction appropriate to each piece and keep it clear of hot heatsinks. [Prusa PETG guidance](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/petg_2059)

## Push performance and power

- **Mass budget.** Native part volumes imply about 2.43 kg of modeled steel, 0.37 kg of printed parts and spacers at solid-equivalent density, and 0.45 kg for tread and rims. Six motors at 320 g add 1.92 kg. Using 66 g per driver, 135 g per pack and 40 g for logic gives 5.62 kg before hardware, wiring and restraints. A 0.2–0.4 kg allowance puts the first-build estimate near **5.8–6.0 kg**, pending actual pack and printed part weights. The simplified model places the center of mass roughly 11 mm aft of the center axle and 29 mm above the floor datum before adding that allowance. Check it by weighing the completed chassis and each axle pair. [Motor supplier mass](https://electrobes.com/product/jgb37-550-dc-12v-160rpm-high-torque-metal-gear-dc-motor/)
- **Speed.** π × 0.057 × 160/60 = **0.478 m/s** nominal no-load at the rated 12 V. Measure loaded speed, left/right bias and turn-in current. [Motor supplier, SKU 1197](https://electrobes.com/product/jgb37-550-dc-12v-160rpm-high-torque-metal-gear-dc-motor/)
- **Traction.** At a sensitivity case of 5 kg, effective μ = 0.5, 0.8 and 1.1 give 24.5, 39.2 and 54.0 N. With six equally loaded driven contacts and 28.5 mm radius, that requires 0.116, 0.186 and 0.257 N·m per wheel. At 6 kg and μ = 0.8 the result is 47.1 N and 0.224 N·m. Actual push force is bounded by grip and available motor force. Begin at natural mass, measure drawbar force, then add low secured ballast only while measured performance improves and the current path can support it.
- **Wheel loading.** Confirm all six tires touch and weigh the axle pairs on a level surface. A 40 N horizontal reaction at 20 mm above the ground transfers about 6.5 N between outer axle stations in a two-support model (124 mm spacing). Check front contact under pushing, and record pivot current separately from straight pulls. Six fixed wheels scrub during turns.
- **Current and heat.** The vendor's 1.8 A no-load figure implies 5.4 A per side unloaded. The chat's 6 A stall estimate remains unverified. The BTS7960's 43 A typical limit is a chip specification. At 18 A an ideal 16 mΩ path dissipates about 5.2 W, at 30 A about 14.4 W, before switching, PCB, connector and thermal effects. Record current, bus voltage and temperature for controlled starts, pivots and short representative pushes, with an abort control and no sustained locked rotor. A full 3S pack is 12.6 V. Verify rated voltage, duty limit, full-pack braking and current behavior. [Infineon BTS7960 datasheet](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/10/57/infineon-bts7960-ds-en.pdf)
- **Insulation.** Keep the two pack positives separate, with rated protection and accessible disconnects for both. Each drive loop returns to its own pack. Provide the proper signal reference for single-ended inputs or use isolation, and keep traction current out of the signal ground and chassis. Use a dedicated regulator, PCB insulation and terminal covers, grommets at steel edges, strain relief, and a padded strap per LiPo. Verify logic thresholds and fail-safe disable with signal loss, MCU reset and either pack disconnected. Keep the antenna clear of the steel per the actual ESP32 board guidance.

## Release checks

1. Measure the assembled motor, wheel and hardware stack. Record the face pattern, usable screw depth, terminal reach, actual wheel runout and required clamp pad thickness. Fit one rail coupon and prove retention before cutting the full rails.
2. Measure both packs with leads, driver terminals, actual MCU and regulator. Verify power disconnect access and loom paths. Finalize the brackets, standoffs and panel joints around the measured hardware.
3. Confirm clearances under load and through turns. Bench-test the two sides, record the loaded push and temperature data, and choose ballast placement by axle loads and current margin.
4. Bend a same-stock coupon, finalize the scoop joints and recheck the installed toe. Gauge the bot with wheels and fasteners installed. Release laser-cut flats and print parts after these checks.

## CAD verification

SOLIDWORKS Premium 2025 SP2.0 rebuilt and saved the assembly with 84 top-level instances and 27 distinct part files. The measured model box was **292 × 290 × 106 mm** (length × width × height). The native interference run checked all instances and reported 78 interface overlaps. Six are round shaft proxies against the interference-fit D-bores. The other 72 are small local overlaps of the silicone tread envelopes with the six rims. The check reported no clashes involving the chassis or electronics envelopes. Dynamic flex, hardware and cable sweeps require a fit test.
