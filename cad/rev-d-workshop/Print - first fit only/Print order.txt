RAC McLaren sumobot | Motor holder fit test

Please print one copy of 01 PRINT ONE in PETG. The part is 60.75 x 43.3 x 32 mm.
Place one flat axial end on the build plate so the motor bore is vertical.
Start at 0.20 mm layers, 4 to 5 perimeters, 5 top and bottom layers,
and roughly 30% infill in the thicker regions. Check the two floor holes,
the original jaw hole, and washer seats in the slicer. Use local supports
where the slicer preview requires them and leave the sleeve slit open.
Please return the material and slicer estimate with the first sample.

The tray and electronics deck files are optional fit prints after the team
has gauged the real packs and PCBs. They are not part of this one-piece order.
