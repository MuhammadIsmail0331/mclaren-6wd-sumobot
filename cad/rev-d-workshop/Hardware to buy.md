# Fastener counter list

Measure screw length from under the head. Take an actual holder, gearbox and wheel to check fit. The numbers below are for the current workshop configuration.

| Ask for | Installed count | Use and first-fit check |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Steel M4×20 button-head socket screws, M4 nylon-insert locknuts | 18 each | Twelve floor bolts and six separate jaw bolts. Buy 20–24 if the sample fits. Floor heads should be about 2.2 mm high or lower. Take matching Allen key and small open-ended spanner. |
| M4 flat washers, Ø9 mm OD; narrow M4 washers, Ø8 mm OD; Ø8 mm OD M4 clearance spacer stack, 2.0 mm total | 12 floor washers, 6 jaw upper washers, 6 spacer stacks | Use the 2 mm spacer stack directly below the lower jaw bolt head. Two suitable small washers may make the stack if their measured thickness totals 2 mm. Verify full locknut engagement and that the upper tip clears the pack panel. |
| Steel M5×12 low-profile screws, wide flat washers and locknuts | Up to 21 sets in baseline | Eight rail-to-floor, five scoop feet, and eight guard/angle joints. One sample set first. Final scoop backing and corner joints can alter counts or grip lengths. M5 bolt heads below the floor must clear the ring under load. |
| Low-head M3×5 machine screws and ~0.5 mm M3 flat washers | Up to 36, only after one motor gauge | The nominal gearbox uses six per motor. Confirm actual thread, depth, engagement and head fit behind the tire first. |
| M3×6 or ×8 screws, with eight 35 mm M3 female–female nylon spacers | 16 screws and 8 spacers | Pack access panels. Short screws from both ends substitute for the original long M3×50 clearance-sleeve scheme. Match screw length to the actual thread depth and panel thickness. Use separate padded straps for battery retention. |
| M3×12 machine screws, nylon washers/spacers ~4 mm, nuts | 8 each | Driver modules, after checking holes and PCB stack. If the actual holes are M2 or 2.5 mm, choose the matching insulated hardware and adjust the deck template. |
| M4×60 screws, rigid 45 mm M4 clearance sleeves, washers and locknuts | 4 sets | Electronics deck-to-floor. A rigid 40+5 mm clearance sleeve stack can substitute if its ends are flat and the bolt fits. Keep deck conductive hardware clear of PCB pads. Verify underside ground clearance. |
| M3×8 or M3×10 screws, nuts and removable cover tabs | About 8–12 sets | Upper panels after trial fit. Short M3 you already own may work here. Short M2 is for suitably sized small electronics, not the steel rail or motor feet. |
| Two padded battery straps, foam, edge grommets and strain relief | 2 straps plus bench stock | Independent LiPo retention and cable protection. Check shaft retention hardware and mold fasteners with the tire team. |

The Pakistani fastener catalog below lists M4×20 and M5×12 button heads. Availability in a nearby shop and actual head dimensions need confirmation. College Road Alpha Electronics lists threaded nylon M3 spacers up to 35 mm. Electrobes lists XT60 connectors and an LM2596 buck module for the logic supply. Pick up motor-rated wire, fuse holders and suitable isolation hardware only after checking connector fit and electrical ratings.
