RAC McLaren 6WD | Club build prints

P01 Shaheer long holder: PETG, one fit sample first, 6 total after approval.
Size: 60.75 x 43.3 x 75.5 mm. Set the flat open gearbox end on the bed,
with the bore vertical and the rear stop tabs pointing upward.
Start at 0.20 mm layers, 4 or 5 walls, 5 top and bottom layers and
20 to 30 percent infill in thick regions. Inspect jaw holes, floor holes,
washer seats and the rear tabs in the slicer. Use local support where
the preview requires it. Keep the jaw slit open. Check grip and heat with
one actual motor and wheel before printing the rest.

P02 LiPo tray: 2 PETG, 42 x 110 x 10 mm each. Print flat on the base.
P03 Electronics tray: 1 PETG, 109 x 192 x 10 mm. Print flat on the base.
For both trays use 3 walls and 4 or 5 top and bottom layers. Keep the
strap slots open. All parts fit the 220 x 220 x 250 mm printer.

PETG is the club build material for the loaded motor sleeves. The two
tray designs are flat and use no purchased standoffs. Only print the
single P01 for the first holder test order.
