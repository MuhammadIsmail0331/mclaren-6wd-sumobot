# Lower-cost Club chassis

Saved SolidWorks 2025 fit prototype: 108 fixed instances, 25 referenced part files,
six long holders, 1 mm welded steel frame, strapped batteries and a tied electronics
tray. The earlier Workshop revision remains available separately.

The rails, guards, rear corners and scoop support joints rely on fixtured welding.
Tape and ties secure the electronics, not the motor/steel load path. No purchased
spacers or PCB standoffs are required for this configuration. The long holder uses
two floor bolts and one active clamp bolt per motor; a four-floor-hole revision is
still outstanding. Verify real gearbox screws and a complete motor station first.

Envelope: 294 x 290 x 102 mm. This is not a 250 mm-square robot. Current competition
eligibility, loaded clearance, weld quality, heat and impact behaviour remain open.
