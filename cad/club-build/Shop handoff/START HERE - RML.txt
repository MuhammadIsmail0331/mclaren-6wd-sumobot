McLaren 6WD Club | RML cut and bend order

Cut from measured 1.0 mm weldable steel. Import DXFs at 1:1 in mm.
C01 must read 250 x 288 mm. Use the photos for part IDs and quantities.
Cut one T01 test piece and one C04 doubler first. Test the holes, strap slot and 90-degree bend.
Reuse the successful C04 as one of the six. Transfer-drill the actual motor screw pattern after fit.
Check the scoop on a same-stock test strip. If the brake develops a different blank, have the shop
adjust the bent profiles before batch cutting. Get supervisor approval before changing stock to 0.8 mm.
Use the second picture for bend directions and the motor rail-to-base joint.
