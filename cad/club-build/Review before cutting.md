# Manual review before you send files

Keep the professional Workshop and Club folders separate. The club build is the lower-cost revision. Start with the club assembly and use the professional Workshop assembly as the comparison.

| Area | Professional Workshop | Club build | What to check yourself |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motor mounts | 32 mm sleeves with separate jaw and floor screws | 72.5 mm can coverage, original bore, split and stop tabs, 75.5 mm overall | Check six in plan view. Check terminals and vents on one real motor. Install only the outboard jaw screw. |
| Base | 52 round holes for mounts and bolted subassemblies | 12 mount holes and four strap slots | The 12 round holes match six mounts with two each. Check the four slots against the two identical trays. |
| Gearbox face | Six nominal screws per motor | Three widely spaced screws proposed, located by transfer drilling after the real-motor coupon | Verify boss, mounting thread, safe depth and screw head clearance behind the tire. |
| Steel joints | Bolted rail returns, bolt-on side brackets, separate scoop feet and upper covers | One-piece folded rails and guards, welded rear corners, one folded scoop on five webs, welded central spine | Ask the shop to fixture both axles, keep the floor flat, and fit the five webs against the actual formed nose. |
| Electronics | Purchased spacers, screws and separate covers | One PETG tray on a bent steel bridge, with ties and tape | Check that the driver undersides have an air gap, the wires have strain relief and the plugs remain reachable. |
| Batteries | Two trays, access covers and spacer stacks | Two identical printed trays and two straps anchored directly through steel | Check both packs and lead exit direction. Each tray allows a modeled 86 × 38 × 23 mm pack body. |
| Fasteners | Multiple M3, M4, M5 lengths and standoffs | One M4×20 size for all mount bolts, plus verified short gearbox screws | Compare the screw heads and washer sizes to the shopping list. |

Check the physical rim before drilling. The Club uses the approximately 40 × 33 mm rim model, and the other team STEP is 41 × 37 mm.

## Shaheer V2 holder comparison

| Feature | Shaheer's original V2 | Club P01 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Floor mounting | Four Ø4.5 mm holes. Two per foot, with 38.45 mm between the two rows along the can. | Two Ø4.5 mm holes on one row, 49 mm apart across the feet. Flat pads and washer seats fit C01 and the C03 backing strips. |
| Overall holder envelope | 84.03 mm wide and 79.9 mm long. | 60.75 mm wide and 75.5 mm long. The 62 mm pitch leaves 1.25 mm between the width envelopes. |
| Can and split | Ø37.4 mm bore, split sleeve and two clamp stations. | Same bore and clamp stations. Install one clamp bolt at the station nearer the wheel. |
| End and foot details | Original end stops and wide, slightly angled feet. | Rear stop tabs with a wiring opening. The lower relief clears the folded rail foot. |

The Club holder was built from a copy of Shaheer's V2 SolidWorks file. The Club mounting changes affect how the feet take load. The CAD check covered geometry and does not establish that Shaheer's calculations for the original feet apply to the Club layout.

Use the two-hole P01 and C01/C03 set in the final Club pack. The three confirmed gearbox face screws into the steel rail and doubler secure the front of the motor. The two M4 foot bolts secure the holder to the floor. Four foot bolts would require a second row of flat washer seats and revised C01/C03 profiles. Check one fully assembled motor station for rocking or slip before releasing the batch.

## In SolidWorks

1. In **Top** view, count three tires and three long holders per side. Measure the closest holder gap and check the orientation of the battery leads.
2. In **Front** or **Right** view, confirm all six tires share the same nominal ground plane. Check the 2 mm scoop toe clearance and the 2.8 mm clearance below the modeled low screw heads.
3. Temporarily hide the scoop and electronics tray to inspect the motor banks. Show them again before saving. The top of the club build is open for service.
4. Check the RML cut list against the steel component labels. The five types marked for bending are the motor rail, wheel guard, rear wall, scoop and electronics bridge. Use the forming map for the bend directions. Confirm the cutter scale using the 250 × 288 mm floor outline.

## Release order

Send one holder STL for a fit print and one C04 gearbox doubler plus T01 to RML for test cuts. Confirm the forming coupon and measured gearbox pattern. Approve the remainder of the cut and print quantities after the fit, clearance and heat checks.
