MATERIAL / SETTINGS
Use PETG for the rims. Suggested starting settings for a 0.4 mm nozzle:
0.20 mm layers, 6 perimeter walls, 6 top and bottom layers, 100% infill.
The torque-bearing hub is deliberately solid. Original outer silicone-key
features are retained, including some locally thin filleted regions.

For moulds use PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers, 20% infill.
Use the filament maker's temperature and cooling settings. Enable bridging.
The small horizontal holes and the 9.4 mm access recess have bridge roofs;
the slicer operator must inspect them. Keep support material out of the hex
socket and mould cavity. No printer-specific G-code is supplied.

T02 tests the hex socket, M4 clearance and retaining-screw seat, not the
complete axle stack. The first P04 is the complete motor/barrel/grub check.
Target P04 socket: 12.4 mm across flats for the supplier's nominal 12 mm hex.
P04 motor-side entry is 15 mm diameter for the barrel and short grub ends.
Use short M4x4 grub screws in the adapter's radial mounting holes; longer
protruding screws can catch the entry bore. Check the supplied screws first.

Do not print the silicone tyre, brass coupler or metal fastener references.
