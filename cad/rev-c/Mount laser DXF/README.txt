REV C MOTOR MOUNT PLATES - millimetres, nominal 1.0 mm steel.

Quantities: 01 x1, 02 x2, 03 x2, 23 x6, 24 x2.
These are flat plates only; 02+03 form each motor rail assembly.
Nominal six-M3 pattern: 31 mm PCD around motor housing centre, which is 6 mm behind the shaft in the supplied motor reference.
GAUGE ONE REAL GEARBOX BEFORE BATCH CUTTING. Where the pattern is uncertain, omit the 3.4 mm holes in 02/23, then transfer-drill through both clamped layers from the actual gearbox.
No kerf offset is included; laser shop sets compensation. Deburr holes and join 02/03 square without warping the floor.
Scoop and other formed-part developments are not included in this motor-mount DXF folder; their bend allowance still needs the shop coupon.
