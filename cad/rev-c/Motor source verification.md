# Electrobes JGB37-550 12 V 160 RPM - supplier check

Checked 7 November 2026 against the exact product the user bought:
https://electrobes.com/product/jgb37-550-dc-12v-160rpm-high-torque-metal-gear-dc-motor/

| Item | Electrobes listing |
|---|---|
| Supply and no-load speed | 12 V, 160 RPM |
| Shaft | 6 mm D shaft, 14.5 mm long |
| Body size in description | 76.5 mm long x 36.5 mm diameter |
| Total length including shaft | 105 mm |
| Body size in Specification tab | 76.5 mm x 31 mm |
| Listed mass | 320 g |

The two diameter values are inconsistent. The listing does not establish the front mounting-hole coordinates, their exact thread engagement limit or shaft-to-housing eccentricity. It therefore cannot justify treating the old simplified motor STEP as an exact model of the delivered units.

Rev C uses the team's imported 36.8 mm body reference and a 37.0 mm prototype clamp bore. If the real body is 36.5 mm as the description says, the nominal diameter clearance is 0.5 mm rather than the 0.2 mm modeled against the old reference. Keep the current clamp as a one-pair fit trial. Verify the body and front interface before a six-mount print or full rail batch. A dimensioned supplier drawing or measurements from one physical motor can resolve this without another CAD import.

This is a supplier-data review. The Rev C geometry remains a prototype until the delivered motor interface is checked.
