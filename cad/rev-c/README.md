# McLaren 6WD — Rev C motor mount prototype

Open **McLaren 6WD - Rev C.SLDASM** with all 36 .SLDPRT files in this folder. Keep `Reference STEP` with the CAD package. Rev A and Rev B remain in the repository history and adjacent CAD folders.

Also read **Motor source verification.md** for the Electrobes dimension conflict.

Start with **Motor mount review.md** and **Views/Motor mount dimensions.png**. This revision uses a steel gearbox face rail/doubler plus a printed saddle and bolted cap. Confirm the real motor's body diameter, eccentricity, front pattern and safe thread depth. Print one clamp pair before batching six.

- `Print STL`: eight unique printable parts, in millimetres. Quantities are in `Parts and quantities.csv`. STL export uses the SolidWorks positive-space translation; orient the motor clamp on its axial end in the slicer.
- `Mount STEP`: individual STEP exports of the print parts and revised mount plates.
- `Mount laser DXF`: five flat motor/floor plate profiles, in millimetres, with quantities and pattern-gauging instructions. Formed scoop developments still need the brake-shop coupon and bend allowance.
- `Views`: complete, service, drivetrain, motor-bank and individual-part images.
- `Build and shopping list.md`: complete inherited chassis fabrication guide, updated for Rev C.
- `Verification`: native rebuild/interference results, saved flags, mesh measurements and orientation comparisons.

The native assembly reopened successfully. All 36 part documents and the assembly reported no pending saved changes. The CAD envelope is 292 × 290 × 110 mm. This is a positioned fit prototype, with physical motor fit, loaded clearance, material and joint tests still required before competition use.
