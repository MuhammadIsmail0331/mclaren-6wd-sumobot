FIRST DRY TEST - BEFORE MIXING SILICONE
1. Check the real brass adapter slides into the rim, the supplied grub screws
   clear the 15 mm entry, and the wheel can slide off again. Fit the adapter to
   the motor first, with a grub bearing on the shaft flat; then slide on the rim.
2. The outside screw recess faces DOWN on the mould base. Motor/hex side faces UP.
   Slide the rim over the 4.2 mm printed pin gently. Its clearance hole is 4.5 mm.
   Lightly clean the hole if necessary; do not force the pin or bend it sideways.
3. Fit the wall around the rim and onto the base. Check it sits flat and remains
   concentric. Temporarily secure it to the base with tape around the outside.
4. Mark a fill line 33 mm above the base TOP, aligned with the rim's top face.
   The main mould wall is 36 mm tall: DO NOT fill to its top or the 8 mm tabs.
5. Mask the central hub recesses with clean tape, sealing around the pin, to keep
   silicone out of the screw/hex/barrel seats. Leave the outer tyre-grip slots open.
   Seal the OUTSIDE wall/base joint. Use a silicone-compatible release on mould
   surfaces only, not on the rim's tyre-grip features. Mix/cure as the material
   manufacturer specifies.
6. After full cure, lift the wall vertically off the base, then gently push the
   soft tyre out axially. This supplied wall is ONE continuous ring, not two
   separable halves. Do not lever against the slender pin or force the notches.
   Lift the wheel straight off the pin. If demoulding is difficult, stop before
   bending/breaking the pin and improve the tool before making the next five.
