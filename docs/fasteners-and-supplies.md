## Which mould is being made?

The full quantities above include the **15 November split mould** with metal
centre pin. The **17 November V3 supplied mould** instead has a printed centre
pin and one continuous wall. For that V3 test set, omit the mould-only two M4 x
20 bolts, one M4 x 50 bolt, three plain nuts and six 9 mm washers. Robot hardware
does not change. Thus the V3-tool route needs 18 installed M4 x 20 bolts and 18
installed 9 mm washers; buying 24 bolts and 30 washers still leaves useful spares.
No extra mould bolts are needed for the V3 printed-pin tool.

## Fit details

- Floor bolt head height: at most 2.2 mm for the modeled underside clearance.
- Wheel screw head: at most 9 mm diameter and 4 mm high, flat underside; no
  countersunk head. Wheel/mould washers: measured 1 mm thick, 9 mm OD, about
  4.4 mm hole. A usual M4 washer may be 0.8 mm; check the actual thickness.
- Earlier split-mould centre-bolt head: at most 8.4 mm wide and 3.2 mm high.
- The supplier lists two grub screws per adapter, so six packets should provide
  12. Reuse suitable included grubs; do not order another set automatically.
  The recessed wheel entry was checked against short M4 x 4 grubs. Check that
  the delivered screw ends clear it. A headed retaining screw is a separate item
  unless physically present in the packet.
- V3 rim: 4.5 mm web + 1 mm washer with M4 x 10 gives nominal 4.5 mm engagement
  in the reference. The older integrated rim has a 4 mm web and nominal 5 mm.
  Check the real coupler for shaft/screw bottoming before tightening.
- No standoffs, purchased spacers, heat-set inserts or M2/M5 structural screws
  are specified for the saved long-holder Club build.

Also reuse/source two battery straps, six securing cable ties plus wiring ties,
foam tape, padding, electrical insulation and smooth slot/edge protection.
The rails and main steel joints are welded; tape is for electronics mounting.
Fuses, wire, connectors and disconnects must follow measured branch current.
