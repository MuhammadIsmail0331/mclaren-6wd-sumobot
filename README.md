# McLaren 6WD Sumobot

A RAC chassis project for remote-controlled robot sumo. The fit assembly uses six JGB37-550 gearmotors, custom silicone tires, two IBT-2 drivers and two 3S LiPo packs. Each driver and pack serve one side. Rev B uses a 1.0 mm steel load path and bolted PETG motor clamps.

Download a complete CAD bundle from [Releases](https://github.com/MuhammadIsmail0331/mclaren-6wd-sumobot/releases). Extract it and open `McLaren 6WD - Rev B.SLDASM` in SolidWorks 2025 with its parts and `Reference STEP` folder alongside it. The STEP assembly is available for other CAD tools.

Rev B measures **292 × 290 × 110 mm** in CAD. It targets the AIR NEXUS 12 × 12 in footprint. The repo records a digital fit check. The real mass, stock and bend development, electrical performance, and ring clearance still need bench and shop checks before fabrication.
